    How this works

    Starts from combined saved investment-account totals, with cash counted once. Archived copies, checking, savings, credit cards, CSV-only imports, and non-USD accounts are excluded. This is not overall net worth. Reloading reads saved totals; it does not sync providers.

    One assumed return applies to the entire portfolio, including cash. Returns compound monthly and are reinvested; contributions arrive at month-end. The starting monthly amount continues until a scheduled change; changes apply in date order and stay in effect until the next change. A year 1, month 1 change overrides the starting contribution. Amounts stay fixed in nominal dollars between changes. Changes beyond the horizon are excluded and flagged. Monthly rate = (1 + annual return)^(1/12) − 1. Today’s dollars = future value ÷ (1 + inflation)^years.

    No historical return is inferred. This model excludes volatility, withdrawals, taxes, fees, and allocation changes. Starting value + deposits is not a record of historical contributions.

    MyPocket reads saved totals locally. Assumptions stay on this page and clear when you leave; no outside service or trading is involved.
